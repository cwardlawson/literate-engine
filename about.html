About Beckett Beans

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Beckett Beans started as a small family-owned coffee shop with a simple mission: to serve the best coffee in town while creating a welcoming community space.

Our founder, Samuel Beckett, was inspired by his travels through coffee-growing regions of Central and South America. He wanted to bring that authentic coffee experience back home.

Today, we continue that tradition by:

  • Sourcing beans directly from small farmers
  • Roasting in small batches weekly
  • Training our baristas in the art of coffee making
  • Supporting local bakeries for our pastries

Our Values

Quality First

We never compromise on quality. Every bean is carefully selected and every cup is crafted with care.

Community Focused

Beckett Beans is more than just a coffee shop - it's a gathering place for our community.

Sustainability

We are committed to sustainable practices including compostable cups and ethical sourcing.
